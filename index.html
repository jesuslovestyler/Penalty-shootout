How to play

  • Your kick: press on the ball and drag upward — the drag direction aims your shot and the length sets the power. Let go to shoot. Beat the keeper, but don't drag so hard you balloon it over the bar.
  • Their kick: you're in goal. When the shooter winds up, tap the left, center, or right of the goal to dive that way. Match the shot's side to save it.
  • The shootout: five kicks each, taking turns. If one side can't catch up, it ends early. Level after five? It's sudden death — first pair of kicks that splits, wins.
